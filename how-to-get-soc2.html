How to get a SOC 2 report

A SOC 2 Type II report is an independent audit of a vendor's security controls, and it is the clearest evidence that a service handles data responsibly. There is no central registry of these reports, and vendors typically release them only to account holders. Because you already work with the vendor, you are usually best positioned to obtain the report and upload it with your request.

Why the requestor provides the report. There is no directory in which to look these up, many vendors release the report only to an account holder, and some vendors do not have one. Rather than have ITS request a report from a vendor with which it may have no relationship, the requestor obtains and uploads it. If you are unable to obtain the report, indicate that on the form and ITS will determine the appropriate next step.

1Locate the vendor's Trust Center or security page

This is where most vendors publish their compliance documentation. You can usually find it by searching the web for:

  • "[vendor name] trust center", "[vendor name] SOC 2", or "[vendor name] security"
  • Common addresses take the form trust.vendor.com or vendor.com/security

The page typically lists the certifications the vendor holds (for example, SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001) and provides a means to request the report.

2Obtain the report

  • Most trust centers ask you to enter your Syracuse email address and accept a brief non-disclosure agreement, after which they provide the report as a PDF.
  • If you have an account with the product, check its administration or compliance settings, where some vendors make the report available to account holders.
  • If neither option is available, request the report from the vendor's support team or your account contact.
Upload the report file with your request. Download the report as a PDF and upload it on the request form. Please provide the file itself rather than a link: ITS often cannot access a vendor portal that requires an account, whereas you can.

3If you cannot obtain the full report

Indicate this on the form. The following public sources may still assist ITS in its review:

  • CSA STAR Registry (cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry) - a free, public listing; note whether the vendor appears and at what level.
  • A SOC 3 report - a public summary that some vendors publish on their security page.
  • An ISO 27001 certificate - if the vendor claims one, it can often be verified publicly.

4If the vendor does not have a SOC 2 report

This is common for smaller or open-source tools and is not an automatic denial. Indicate it on the form. ITS will consider alternatives such as an ISO 27001 certificate or a Higher Education Community Vendor Assessment Toolkit (HECVAT) response, and in some cases a local connection in Claude Desktop, which keeps data on your own machine, may be more appropriate.

What to include with your request:
  • the SOC 2 Type II report (PDF), uploaded to the form; or
  • a note that you were unable to obtain the report; or
  • a note that the vendor does not have one - whichever applies.
